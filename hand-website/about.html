About

Websites are projects to everyone else. To us, they're a responsibility.

Why HAND exists

Too many good businesses are held back by bad websites.

HAND started with a pattern we kept seeing: excellent businesses — skilled, trusted, busy — represented online by something that undersold everything they'd built. Not because they didn't care, but because nobody had ever taken real ownership of their presence.

The agency would launch and leave. The freelancer would drift away. The "website person" would change jobs. And the site would quietly decay while the business it represented kept getting better.

So we built the company we kept wishing existed: one partner, permanently accountable, for everything your business is online. Handcrafted work, hands-on care — your presence in good hands. That's the name, and that's the whole idea.
