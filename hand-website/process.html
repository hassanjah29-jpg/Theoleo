Process

From First Conversation to Long-Term Growth

A clear, structured process means no surprises — just consistent delivery. Here's exactly what working with HAND looks like.

01

Discovery

A 2–3 hour strategy workshop. We learn your business, goals, competitors and users — before anything is created.

Client involvement: High · 1–2 weeks
02

Strategy

Digital strategy document, sitemap, information architecture, SEO keyword strategy and a KPI framework.

Client involvement: Review & approve · 1 week
03

Planning

Project plan, content brief, design brief and technical specification. A clear roadmap — no more 'how long will this take?'

Client involvement: Timeline review · 3–5 days
04

Design

Wireframes, visual design for desktop and mobile, an interactive prototype and a full design system.

Client involvement: 2 review rounds · 2–4 weeks
05

Development

Staging site build, CMS setup, integrations, and performance optimisation. You watch it come to life.

Client involvement: Weekly updates · 3–8 weeks
06

Testing

Cross-browser, device, performance, accessibility and security testing. Nothing surprises you post-launch.

Client involvement: Acceptance testing · 1 week
07

Launch

DNS migration, SSL, analytics live, Search Console submission, redirects — then 30 days of monitoring.

Client involvement: Go/no-go approval · 1–2 days
08

Maintenance

Updates, security scans, uptime monitoring and content hours. You never think about your website going down.

Client involvement: Monthly report · Ongoing
09

Growth

SEO reporting, CRO recommendations, A/B tests and content publishing. Your site gets better every month.

Client involvement: Monthly strategy call · Ongoing
