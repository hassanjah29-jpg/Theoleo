The HAND Method

A named process. Because vague promises aren't a plan.

You've probably heard "we have a proven process" before. Here is ours, stage by stage — what happens, what you get, and exactly how much of your time it takes.

Discovery — week 0

We begin with questions, not quotes. A 30-minute call, an honest audit of your current presence, and a mutual fit decision. If we're not the right partner, we'll say so and point you somewhere better. Your time: 30 minutes.

Strategy — weeks 1–2

A working session with your team, audience and competitor research, messaging, sitemap, and a conversion plan. You'll know the full timeline and investment before anything is built. Your time: 90 minutes.

Design — weeks 3–4

Design direction, then the full design — reviewed at three fixed checkpoints. You're consulted at the moments that matter, not buried in revision rounds. Your time: 3 × 30 minutes.

Development — weeks 4–6

We build to measurable standards: fast load times, accessibility, SEO foundations, and a CMS you can actually use. You'll see progress on a staging link the whole way. Your time: none required.

Launch — week 6–8

Managed migration, zero-downtime cutover, training for your team, and our launch-date guarantee: we commit to a date and we hit it. Your time: a 45-minute handover.

Care — ongoing

Hosting, security, backups, updates, and monthly content hours under a 24-hour response SLA. Launch is day one, not goodbye. Your time: send an email when you want something changed.

Growth — ongoing

Monthly reports, quarterly growth reviews, SEO and optimization roadmaps. Your website gets measurably better every quarter — and you see the numbers. Your time: one 30-minute review per quarter.

Total client time before launch: about three hours. We do the work. That's what you're paying for.

Fit

Who we're for — and who we're not.

Probably not a fit
