ClientHarbor & Slate Law
IndustryProfessional Services
ServicesStrategy · Design · SEO · Care
Timeline8 weeks to launch
Headline result+212% qualified leads

The situation

A respected two-partner firm whose reputation was built on referrals — and whose website looked like it. By the time a referred prospect finished "checking them out online," the polish of the in-person experience had already been undercut.

The problem

The site took six seconds to load, didn't work on phones, and was invisible for every search a potential client might actually type. It answered no questions and asked for nothing.

The approach

We rebuilt the firm's presence around the questions clients ask before hiring a lawyer — fees, process, outcomes — and gave every page one job: start a conversation. Local SEO put the firm on the map for the searches that matter.

Eighteen months later, we still meet quarterly. Leads are up another 40% on year one, and the firm has added two associates to handle the volume.

ClientNorthpoint HVAC
IndustryHome Services
ServicesDesign · Local SEO · Care
Timeline6 weeks to launch
Headline result3.4× booked calls

The situation

Twelve trucks, twenty years in business, and a feast-or-famine lead flow that depended entirely on word of mouth and a fading spot in a paper directory.

The problem

Searching "furnace repair" in their own service area brought up three competitors before them. Their site had no way to book, no reviews, and no reason to call.

The approach

A conversion-first rebuild: click-to-call everywhere, service-area pages for every town they cover, review integration, and a Google Business Profile overhaul. Then a Care Plan, because an HVAC owner has no time to think about websites — that's the point.

The result

3.4×
booked calls within 5 months
#1
map-pack ranking in 6 of 8 towns
0 hrs
owner time spent on the website since launch

The partnership

Two winters in, seasonal campaign pages go up every October without Ray ever asking. "The phone rings now. That's the whole review."

ClientOlive & Ash
IndustryHospitality
ServicesBranding · Design · Care · Analytics
Timeline7 weeks to launch
Headline result+68% direct reservations

The situation

A restaurant whose dining room was unforgettable and whose website was a PDF menu from 2019. Nearly every reservation arrived through third-party platforms — along with their commissions.

The problem
