FAQ

Questions, Answered Honestly

If your question isn't here, ask us directly — we reply within one business day.

How long does a website project typically take?

Starter projects launch in as little as 3–4 weeks. Growth-tier builds typically take 6–10 weeks; complex platforms 8–12+. We give you an exact timeline before you commit, and we hit it.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped individually, but our packages start with a clear 'from' price so you know where you stand before a call. A poorly built website costs more in the long run — we'll always show you the most cost-effective path, even if it's smaller than you expected.

Do you work with small businesses or only large companies?

Both. Our Starter package serves new businesses building their first serious presence; our Enterprise tier serves established companies with complex needs.

What happens after my website launches?

We don't disappear. Every project includes a 30-day support window, and most clients move onto a maintenance or growth retainer — 84% of our clients are on one.

Do I need to provide content and copy?

No. Conversion copywriting and content strategy are part of what we do. You provide the business knowledge; we turn it into copy that converts.

How is HAND different from hiring a freelancer?

Continuity, accountability and full scope. A freelancer covers one discipline and may vanish post-launch. HAND covers strategy, design, development, SEO, hosting and maintenance — as a team that stays.

Will my website be fast and mobile-friendly?

Guaranteed. Your website will score 90+ on Google PageSpeed Insights — or we fix it, free. Every design starts mobile-first.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. Redesigns, speed optimisation, SEO, security hardening and maintenance plans all work with sites we didn't build.

Do you offer ongoing SEO?

Yes — it's one of our core retainers. SEO compounds: most clients see meaningful movement in 3–4 months and significant results by month 6–9.

I've been burned by agencies before. Why should I trust you?

We hear this from nearly every client. Here's what we do differently: weekly updates without being asked, senior people doing the work, published retention metrics, and a free audit before you spend a penny — so you can judge our thinking first.
