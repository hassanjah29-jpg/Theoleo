FAQ

Honest answers to fair questions.

How much does a website cost?

Most HAND projects land between $10,000 and $50,000 depending on scope, with Care Plans from a few hundred dollars per month and growth retainers scaled to ambition. We publish ranges because "it depends — book a call" wastes your time. If your budget is under $10k, a template-based option may genuinely serve you better, and we'll tell you so on the discovery call.

Will I be locked in to you?

No — and this is in writing in every contract. You own everything: code, content, design files, domains, and accounts, all in your name from day one. If you ever leave, we'll help you migrate. Clients stay because they want to; 94% are still with us after year one.

What happens after launch?

Launch is day one, not goodbye. Every project moves into a Care Plan — hosting, security, backups, updates, and monthly content hours under a 24-hour response SLA. Most clients add quarterly growth reviews, where we look at the numbers and decide what to improve next.

How long does a project take?

Most sites launch in six to eight weeks from the strategy kickoff. You'll have the exact date before we start — and our launch-date guarantee means that if we miss it, you don't pay the final installment until we're done.

How much of my time will this take?

About three hours total before launch: a discovery call, a strategy session, three short design reviews, and a handover. We structured the process so you can delegate the outcome instead of managing the project.

We already have a website. Do we have to start over?

Not necessarily. The free audit will tell you honestly whether your current site is worth improving or genuinely holding you back. Sometimes the right answer is a focused round of fixes plus a Care Plan — and when it is, that's what we'll recommend.

What makes HAND different from other agencies?

The model. Most agencies are built around projects: pitch, build, launch, goodbye. HAND is built around partnership: the majority of our work is the ongoing care, optimization, and growth of sites we've launched. That changes the incentives — we build things we'll have to maintain, and our best marketing is your year-two results.

Do you work with businesses in any industry?

We focus where we're strongest: professional services, home services and trades, healthcare, hospitality, and B2B manufacturing. If you're outside those, the discovery call will sort out fit quickly — and if we're wrong for you, we'll point you toward someone better suited.
