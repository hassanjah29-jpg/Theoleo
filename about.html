Our Story

One Scoop Was Never Enough.

Second Scoop started with a simple, slightly greedy idea: the best part of a cookie is the warm, gooey middle — so why stop at one bite?

The beginning

It started in a tin.

We were chasing one texture: the moment a cookie comes out of the oven still soft enough to scoop. Too good to wait for, too gooey to hold. So we leaned all the way in and baked it in a tin — the OG Scoopie — made to be eaten warm, with a spoon, straight from the centre.

That first scoop disappeared fast. The second one is why we're here.

🥄
The standard

Premium, not precious.

Real butter, Belgian chocolate, proper vanilla, a little sea salt. We use ingredients we'd happily eat by the spoonful — because with our edible cookie dough, you literally do. Everything is heat-treated and egg-free where it needs to be, so the only thing you have to worry about is sharing.

No shortcuts, no weird aftertaste. Just dessert that tastes like the memory of dessert.

🍫
The culture

Built around the drop.

We bake in small weekly batches and release limited drops that come and go fast — sometimes in under an hour. Some scoops never make the menu at all; they live in The Vault, unlocked only with a code. It keeps things exciting, a little exclusive, and very shareable.

What grew out of it is a community of people who plan their week around a Friday drop. That's the whole point.
