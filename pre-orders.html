How Pre-Orders Work

Fresh Means Pre-Order.

We don't bake to sit on a shelf. We bake in small weekly batches, to your slot — which means ordering ahead. Here's exactly how it works.

Step by step

From Tap to Spoon

1

Orders open

Each week we open a pre-order window and announce it on the banner and Instagram. Pick your region, browse the lineup, add what you want.

2

Reserve your slot

Check out to reserve. In Pakistan we confirm over WhatsApp; in Toronto you'll get an email confirmation. Payment locks your batch.

3

Orders close

At the cutoff (watch the banner) the window closes so we can finalise quantities and bake everything fresh — nothing pre-made.

4

We bake & hand over

On drop day we bake to your slot, then deliver or hand it over at pickup. Scoopies travel warm-ready — reheat for that molten centre.
