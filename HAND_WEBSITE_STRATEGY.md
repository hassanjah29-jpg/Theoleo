# SECTION 1: BUSINESS POSITIONING

## Unique Value Proposition

> **HAND is the only digital partner that handles your entire online presence — strategy, design, development, and growth — under one roof, with the senior expertise of a boutique agency and the accountability of a long-term partner.**

Most businesses work with a freelancer who disappears, or a bloated agency that overpromises. HAND is neither. We are a focused, senior-led team that becomes a permanent extension of your business — handling everything from your first pixel to your ongoing growth metrics.

---

## Ideal Customer Profiles

### ICP 1 — The Ambitious SMB Owner
- Revenue: $500K–$5M/year
- Problem: Has a bad website or no website strategy; losing leads to better-looking competitors
- Goal: A professional digital presence that generates real business
- Pain: Has been burned by cheap freelancers or slow agencies
- Trigger: Rebranding, funding round, new product launch, expansion

### ICP 2 — The Growth-Stage Startup (Series A/B)
- Revenue: $2M–$20M/year
- Problem: Scrappy MVP site no longer matches brand; needs scalable marketing infrastructure
- Goal: Conversion-optimized website that supports investor credibility and lead generation
- Pain: Engineering team focused on product, not marketing site
- Trigger: Funding event, new CMO, competitive pressure

### ICP 3 — The Enterprise Marketing Director
- Revenue: $20M+ company
- Problem: Needs a specialist partner for a specific digital initiative (redesign, SEO overhaul, landing page system)
- Goal: Reliable, accountable, premium execution with zero micromanagement
- Pain: Internal resources stretched; previous agencies underdelivered
- Trigger: Budget cycle, new campaign, site performance issues

### ICP 4 — The Professional Services Firm
- Industry: Legal, financial, consulting, healthcare, accounting
- Problem: Generic or outdated website fails to convey expertise and convert visitors
- Goal: A credibility-first digital presence that attracts high-value clients
- Pain: Doesn't understand digital; needs a trusted advisor, not just an executor

## Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

### Brand Personality Pillars
- **Confident, not arrogant** — We know our craft deeply and speak with quiet authority
- **Direct, not cold** — We say what we mean without corporate fluff or buzzwords
- **Premium, not pretentious** — Excellence is standard; we don't need to shout about it
- **Partner, not vendor** — We speak in "we" and "your business," not "our services"
- **Educative, not condescending** — We help clients understand without patronising them

### Tone of Voice
- **Headlines:** Bold, benefit-driven, occasionally provocative
- **Body copy:** Conversational but precise; no jargon without immediate explanation
- **CTAs:** Action-oriented, low-pressure, value-first ("Get Your Free Audit" not "Buy Now")
- **Case Studies:** Results-first, data-driven, story-led
- **Error/404:** Human, slightly self-deprecating, immediately helpful

### Words to Use
partnership, outcomes, growth, precision, clarity, trust, proven, senior, dedicated, accountable, results

### Words to Avoid
synergy, leverage (as a verb), disruptive, innovative (without proof), cheap, affordable, best-in-class (without evidence), solutions (generic)

### Best Filtering System
- **Filter by:** Industry | Service | Technology | Result Type
- **Sort by:** Most Recent | Most Impactful | Featured
- **No infinite scroll** — paginate at 12, keeps pages fast and indexable

### Best Layout for Credibility
1. Lead with **outcomes**, not just visuals — results convert better than pretty pictures
2. Show **real device mockups** (not just flat screens)
3. Include **client logos** where permitted
4. Alternate card sizes — one large hero card per row for rhythm
5. **No stock clients** — authentic work only; "Confidential" is fine if results are real

# SECTION 12: CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

## Conversion-Focused Recommendations

### Layouts That Convert

**1. F-Pattern & Z-Pattern Awareness**
- Place the most important CTA in the top-right (where eyes land on desktop)
- Lead with benefit, not feature — "More leads" not "Custom development"
- Use visual hierarchy to guide eye movement to CTAs

**2. The "Inverted Pyramid" Content Structure**
- Every page: most important information at top, detail below fold
- Eliminate scrolling requirements for core value prop
- Put the CTA above the fold on high-intent pages (Pricing, Contact, Free Audit)

**3. Single-Column Mobile Focus**
- 60%+ of traffic will be mobile — design mobile-first
- Stack elements vertically, single CTA per viewport
- Thumb zone optimisation: primary CTA at bottom of screen on mobile

---

### Trust-Building Placements (Psychology)

**Proximity Principle:** Place trust elements (stars, logos, testimonials) immediately adjacent to CTAs — decision anxiety peaks at the moment of action.

**Social Proof Timing:** Show testimonials after describing the problem they solve — not before (context makes social proof 3x more effective).

**Specificity over Superlatives:** "98% client retention" converts better than "world-class service." Numbers are trusted; adjectives are ignored.

---

### CTA Positioning

| Page Location | Recommended CTA Strategy |
|---|---|
| Above fold | One primary CTA only — no competing options |
| Mid-page | Contextual CTA matching surrounding content |
| Bottom of page | Both CTAs (primary + secondary) |
| Sticky nav | CTA always visible regardless of scroll position |
| Exit intent | "Before you go — get your free audit" popup (one-time) |

---

### Form Optimization

**Reduce fields to minimum viable:**
- Audit form: Name, Email, Website, Challenge (4 fields max)
- Contact form: Name, Email, Message (3 fields)

**Micro-copy best practices:**
- Privacy note below email field: "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime."

- Placeholder text: Provide examples, not just labels

**Multi-step forms for complex asks:**
- Step 1: Contact info → Step 2: Project details → Step 3: Budget + timeline
- Progress indicator reduces abandonment (shows commitment progress)

### Layout Principles

- **12-column grid** (1440px max-width, 120px gutters desktop)
- **8px spacing unit** — all spacing is multiples of 8
- **Generous whitespace** — premium brands breathe; cheap brands cram
- **Asymmetric layouts** for visual interest on feature sections (avoid grid monotony)
- **Full-bleed sections** alternate with contained content for rhythm
- **No visible borders** — use shadow and spacing to define containers

---

### Visual Hierarchy

1. One H1 per page (large, bold, immediate impact)
2. One primary CTA per viewport (never competing)
3. High-contrast accent colour used sparingly (one place per section maximum)
4. Pull quotes amplified for scanning readers
5. Result numbers displayed at 3–4× body size
6. Client logos in grayscale (don't compete with brand colour)

---

### Animation Guidelines

**Philosophy:** Purposeful motion, never decorative noise.

**What to animate:**
- Scroll-triggered reveals (fade + translate up, 0.4s ease-out, staggered)
- Counter animations for statistics (trigger on viewport entry)
- CTA hover states (subtle background shift, 0.2s ease)
- Page transitions (cross-fade, 0.3s)
- Navigation dropdown/mobile menu (0.25s ease-in-out)

**What to never animate:**
- Body text on scroll (impedes readability)
- Logos or trust badges (looks amateurish)
- Anything that delays access to content
- Parallax on mobile (causes motion sickness, hurts performance)

**Performance rule:** No animation library > 50KB. Use CSS custom properties + Intersection Observer API first; GSAP only for complex sequences.

---

### Imagery Style

- **Real client work:** Always; mockups on Apple/Samsung devices at angles
- **Team photos:** Natural, professional, candid — not stiff corporate headshots
- **Stock photography:** Absolutely not. Illustrations or abstract visuals only.
- **Illustrations:** Abstract, geometric, representing data/systems/flow — Stripe/Linear aesthetic
- **Screenshots:** High-res, retina-quality, recent browser chrome or no chrome
- **Icons:** Custom icon set or Phosphor Icons — consistent weight (1.5px stroke, 24px grid)

---

### Illustration Style

- **Abstract geometric** forms suggesting systems, networks, and architecture
- Thin line art with gold accent fills
- No flat cartoon people (overused, generic)
- Consider: animated SVG diagrams for the Process section
- 3D elements sparingly — product/device mockups only

---

### Component Design System

**Core Components:**
- Button: Primary (solid accent), Secondary (ghost), Tertiary (text link)
- Card: Service card, Case study card, Testimonial card, Blog post card, Team card
- Form: Input, textarea, select, checkbox, radio — all with focus/error/success states
- Navigation: Desktop (fixed top), mobile (full-screen overlay)
- Badge: Industry tag, service tag, result highlight
- Stat block: Large number + label + optional context
- Testimonial block: Stars + quote + author + photo + company logo
- CTA Banner: Full-width, high contrast, headline + two buttons
